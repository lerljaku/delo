## What's new

- The site is now called **Delo** and will move to mtgdelo.com
- Leaderboards per format: Modern, Limited, Duel Commander, EDH, Legacy, Vintage and Premodern, next to the all-formats leaderboard
- Every tournament has a format and can link to its public event page, so anyone can check that it really took place
- Click any column of the leaderboard to sort by it
- Draws no longer change ratings (many are intentional, e.g. into the top 8), and win rate leaves them out: wins ÷ (wins + losses)
- Players get a rank and a visible Elo after 10 rated matches; until then they are listed as provisional
- New Elo scale made for Magic: a 200-point rating difference now means a 60% chance to win (it was 76%); all ratings were recalculated
- Uploading a new tournament updates only the affected ratings instead of recalculating everything
- Players with a verified profile can record the deck they played in each tournament (account page); decks show on tournament and player pages
- Players with a verified profile can add a coloured flair next to their name
- Memberships: Supporter ($5 once) adds a badge; Diamond ($5/month) adds private match notes, matchups by opponent's deck, CSV export and a custom profile URL
- Upcoming tournaments next to the leaderboard; Diamond members can post them, including recurring ones (every week or every month) that show only their next date and warn when it falls on a public holiday
- When uploading results, pick the tournament from the planned ones to fill in its details
- Support this project page with donation QR codes (EUR and CZK); donors get a 💎 next to their name
- Admin: site visitor statistics (daily visitors, page views, top pages and referring sites), counted without cookies
- Islands: groups of players who play each other (e.g. a country) have their own leaderboards, tournaments and upcoming tournaments; pick yours in the navigation, shown with its icon (e.g. a flag)
- Admin: the Admin link shows how many player claims are waiting
- How ratings are calculated is now part of the Docs page; the site has a 👑 browser icon
- The sign-in page now matches the site; the #1 player gets a 👑 on the leaderboard and their player page
- Admin: merge a misspelled player into the right one; later uploads with the same misspelling go to the right player
- Anyone signed in can upload tournament results; admins can correct or delete them
- Privacy notice, account deletion, and removal of your name from all results on request
