Support this project

Delo is free for everyone. If you'd like to help keep it running, you can send a donation by bank transfer.

Donations are used solely for maintenance expenses: hosting, the domain and other running costs of the site.

Become a Supporter 💎

Write your name as it appears on the leaderboard in the payment message. We'll mark you as a Supporter: a 💎 appears next to your name on the leaderboard and your player page.

Scan a code with your banking app and enter any amount. Marking you as a Supporter is done by hand, so it can take a few days. We use the name and message from the payment only for that; see the privacy notice.
