👑 Upload straight from Eventlink

The mtgdelo Chrome extension uploads an Eventlink tournament in one click, with no copying of pairings and no mtgdelo account needed. It reads the results in your Eventlink tab, so your Eventlink login never leaves your browser.

  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. In Chrome open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  3. Open the tournament in Eventlink, click the 👑 button in the toolbar, check the details and click Upload.

Uploading again is safe: the same results change nothing, and after more rounds the earlier upload is replaced. Events with unfinished matches are refused.
